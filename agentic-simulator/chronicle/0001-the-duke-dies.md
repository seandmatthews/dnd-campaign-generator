# Week 1 — The Doge dies

The old Doge of Ruravaqan died on the third night of the late-autumn market,
without naming an heir and without paying anyone. The bells rang for three
days. The creditors arrived on the fourth. The estate's ledgers have not been
opened, and every faction in the city believes it is owed first.

The city stands on the southern shore of the Bonia archipelago — a port of
Shadia, a merchant republic whose real capital lies elsewhere. Ruravaqan's
great families have long governed themselves, and none more so than in death.
