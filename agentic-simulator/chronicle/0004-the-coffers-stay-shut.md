# Week 4 — The Coffers Stay Shut

**Ruravaqan, Week the Fourth**

The harbor did not reopen. The longshoremen carried their strike from the quays to the customs house, picketed it shut, and turned the Mint's inspectors away at the door. To House Ferrand the word went in plain terms: the harbor reopens the day the back wages are paid, and not before. The Mint, meanwhile, laid its proposal on the table — coffers opened, back pay routed through Ferrand hands, customs collections assigned to Ferrand until the debt discharges — and found no takers. An offer of open coffers carries little from coffers of known shallowness. The offer failed by as narrow a margin as the picket succeeded by.

The Reliquary did not wait on the Mint's arithmetic. It enlisted longshoremen at a hired rate to seize House Ferrand's shipments on the Docks and impound them under church seal until the tithes in arrears are paid in full. The same men who kept the customs house shut spent the week stacking Ferrand crates under that seal. A striking man will still carry, if the payer is sound. The Mint pressed its proposal a second time against the impoundment. It failed worse than the first.

The week's widest margin belonged to the bastard, who spent no coin, having none. The petition asked the Reliquary to convene a formal examination of the Mint's debt instrument — its seal, its hand, its witnesses — before the council weighs it against the petitioner's blood again. The Reliquary granted it. The examination will convene; the ledger records nothing of what the seal, hand, and witnesses will say.

The quietest success belonged to the marsh gang, who are owed nothing and collected none of it. Thirty scattered into the Tanneries in twos and threes — hides hauled for coppers, work begged at the rendering pits — and touched neither procession nor storeroom. They intend to keep it that way until spring. The most disciplined faction in Ruravaqan this week held no coin, no muscle, and no legitimacy.

The coffers stayed shut. The crates stayed sealed. The harbor stayed closed, pending wages.
